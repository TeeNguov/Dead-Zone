Walk over supplies: white/red = health, weapon models = ammunition for that weapon, a vest = armor, a medkit = health, boots = speed, and a yellow bolt = one lightning charge. Speed pickups increase your movement level up to 5. Armor absorbs damage before health. You have five lives per run. Collected supplies respawn elsewhere. Find five antidote ingredients on foot, marked by red vials. Each pickup attracts a bigger wave; the fifth reveals the final boss in the central park. Green acid can be dodged or blocked by buildings and cars, leaving walkable acid puddles for two seconds. Standing in acid burns you; move away or jump over it. Shoot, punch, or blast trees, benches, bins, lamps, hydrants, and planters to clear a path. Collisions bring heat. Keep away from the monsters until the stars fade.
